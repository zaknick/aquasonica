How It Works

Absorption

A specialized lithium chloride hydrogel composite absorbs water vapor directly from the air — effective from 25% relative humidity upward. No condensation required. No cooling needed.

Ultrasonic Extraction

At full saturation, a precisely tuned 110 kHz ultrasonic standing wave creates controlled cavitation, mechanically releasing water from the gel in under 2 minutes. No heat — only sound.

Double-Sided Design

Our central moving reflector doubles output within the same footprint. One mechanism sweeps the standing wave across both surfaces, ensuring 100% extraction coverage at all times.

Cómo Funciona
